## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences of equal priority, reached by the same site:

1. **Public institutions and the legal/education sector in Moldova and the EU** — the National Institute of Justice (INJ), CMDA (Chișinău municipal entrepreneurship centre), the EUROBRIDGE UA-MD cross-border grant programme, DARE-EU. They evaluate slowly, involve several people, and need to see compliance, bilingual capability, data governance and named references before a first conversation. The site is evidence of seriousness.

2. **Mid-sized Moldovan businesses** — Holiston Club (fitness/SPA, Chișinău), PixToon Studio (animation). They decide fast, on demonstration and time-to-launch. The site has to let them try the product and reach a human quickly.

Both read Romanian first; Russian is common; English matters for EU-facing readers. The `/en/` version exists for search visibility and international leads.

## Product Purpose

Applexium builds digital products and provides IT and regulatory consulting for startups and institutions. The site sells three own products and the services practice around them.

**Emmi** is the flagship: one AI agent that answers a business's customers by phone, in the website chat widget, and on Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram — with the same knowledge, personality and languages on every channel. It answers only from the documents the client gives it, and hands off to a human when needed.

Success for the site: an institution requests a consultation, or a business tries the live widget and then makes contact.

## Positioning

The combination a neighbouring agency cannot truthfully copy: a working multi-channel AI agent **already in daily use by public institutions**, delivered by a team that also carries the legal and regulatory side (GDPR, AI Act, compliance-by-design) — and that speaks Romanian and Russian natively, which the large international vendors do not do well for this market.

For Emmi specifically: one brain across five channels, grounded strictly in the client's own documents, self-serve from the client's own dashboard.

## Operating Context

- Clients embed Emmi with a single `<script>` tag on their existing site; it runs isolated and cannot break the host page.
- Institutions run bilingual (RO/RU) or trilingual (RO/RU/EN, plus UK for EUROBRIDGE) support and receive thousands of enquiries.
- Clients administer their own agent through a dashboard: knowledge base, conversation history, leads, callbacks, channels, analytics.
- The Emmi demo agent is embedded live on the Emmi product page — visitors talk to the real product, not a mock-up.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Emmi capabilities that are real and currently shipping** (many are absent from the present site and are the reason for this rebuild):

- Channels: phone (inbound and outbound, with live handoff to a colleague), website chat widget, Instagram Direct, WhatsApp, Telegram — self-service connection from the dashboard.
- Languages: Romanian and Russian spoken; Romanian, Russian, English and Ukrainian in chat. Language is detected from what the customer actually writes or says.
- Answers grounded in the client's own uploaded documents and links; never invented.
- Client-editable knowledge base with a full audit trail of who changed what and when.
- "Knowledge gaps" report: questions the agent handled poorly, answerable in one step, which feeds the answer straight back into the knowledge base.
- Lead capture from conversations, with optional forwarding to the client's CRM.
- Callback requests captured with reason and status tracking.
- Call recordings and full transcripts, searchable, exportable to CSV and PDF.
- Analytics: volume, topics, channels, languages, comparison against the previous period, activity by hour and weekday.
- Notifications by email and Telegram, and a weekly Monday digest in the client's chosen language.
- Guardrails: prompt-injection blocking on inbound messages and redaction of personal data.
- GDPR: export or deletion on request, configurable retention.
- Widget appearance, language picker, avatar and disclaimer are configured per client.
- Every client's data is isolated at the database level.

**Constraints the site must respect:**

- **Do not name the third-party vendors behind the stack.** The present Emmi page has a "technology behind Emmi" section naming specific model, speech, voice and telephony providers; it must go. Describe capability and outcome, never suppliers or architecture.
- Static site: no bundler, no framework, no npm packages, no build step for the pages themselves. Vanilla scripts only.
- Romanian is the source of truth; English is generated by `build-en.py` from `data-en` attributes and `en-meta.json`.
- Mobile-first: every page must be presentable on an iPhone-class viewport.

**Known stale content to correct:** the dashboard is described as showing call cost — per-call spend was removed from the client dashboard, clients pay a flat monthly fee. Product descriptions generally lag the built product.

## Brand Commitments

**Pinned visual direction (owner's decision, 2026-08-08).** Three replacement worlds were built as first-viewport comps and all three were declined. The owner pinned the incumbent register instead — "technological, minimalist, visually beautiful" — with the current applexium.com as a taste reference rather than a template to copy exactly. Named craft bar: **Stripe** (animated diagrams that explain a mechanism) and **Framer** (motion-first, scroll drives a real scene). Also named as liked: **populous.com**, **superpower.com**.

What those four references share, and what therefore binds this site: a full-bleed cinematic first viewport with no cards or boxes in it; a large headline at light-to-regular weight, never a heavy display face; pill-shaped buttons; nearly invisible chrome; and a single dominant light source glowing behind the content. Confidence comes from scale and restraint, not ornament.

Palette may stay in the incumbent family (`--dark-bg #071116`, indigo `#1C1890`, cyan `#1FCDFF`, blue `#245EFE`) or shift moderately within it; it is not frozen. Typefaces Nunito (headings) and Questrial (body) are the incumbent pair. Motion and 3D are explicitly wanted, drawn from Codrops/tympanus techniques, and must be real WebGL/canvas rather than a static imitation — subject to the performance and no-bundler constraints below.

## Product Principles

1. **Show, don't assert.** The live widget is the proof; a visitor who can talk to Emmi needs no statistics. Where a claim cannot be demonstrated or attributed, cut it.
2. **Capability, never supplier.** Say what the product does for the customer. The means are not the customer's concern and are not ours to publish.
3. **Two speeds on one page.** An institution reading for evidence and a business owner deciding in two minutes must both be served without either feeling the page was built for the other.
4. **Romanian is the product, not a translation.** Copy is written in Romanian and reads natively; English follows it.
5. **The site must not outrun the product.** Every feature claimed is one that ships today.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public-sector clients in the EU make accessibility a procurement question; the site already publishes an accessibility statement. Keep keyboard operability, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and honour `prefers-reduced-motion` — motion is decorative here and must never be a barrier.
