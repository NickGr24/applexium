Section 1

In short

applexium.com sets no cookies and uses no traffic analytics, tracking pixels or advertising. Your browser keeps only a few items strictly necessary for the site to work, listed below. That is why we don't ask you to choose between “Accept” and “Refuse”: there is nothing optional to refuse. On your first visit we only show an informational notice.

Section 2

What is stored in your browser

Cookies are small text files a website saves on your device. Similar technologies (Local Storage, Session Storage) work in a comparable way, but their contents are not sent to the server automatically. applexium.com uses only the following:

NameTypeProviderDurationPurpose
applexium:cookie-noticeLocal Storageapplexium.com12 monthsRemembers that you closed the cookie notice (version and date), so it isn't shown on every page
va:visit:<id>Local Storage and Session StorageEmmi widget (applexium.com)Until cleared from the browser / end of sessionRemembers locally when you last visited, so the Emmi assistant doesn't repeat its greeting; never sent to any server

Both entries are strictly necessary for functionality you use, and neither identifies you.

Section 3

Third-party services

The site's fonts and images are hosted on our own domain; we load nothing from advertising or social networks. The Emmi assistant loads from app.emmi-agent.com without cookies; if you open a conversation, it runs in an Emmi platform window as described in the Privacy Policy. The contact form sends data to Formspree only when you submit it.

Section 4

How to control stored data

You can delete the items above at any time in your browser settings (the section on cookies and site data). After that, the informational notice will appear again on your next visit.

If we ever add optional cookies (for example, analytics), we will enable them only after your explicit consent, given through a banner with equal “Accept” and “Refuse” options, and we will update this policy.

Section 5

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the technologies used on the site change. Every version is dated (current version: 2026-10).
