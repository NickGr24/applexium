Section 4

Data Retention Period

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected:

Contact form data: up to 24 months from the date of receipt, or less if the inquiry does not lead to a contractual relationship;

  • Technical data (logs): up to 12 months;

Data included in contracts or accounting documents: as required by Moldovan legal retention obligations.

Upon expiry of the retention period, data is permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

Section 5

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under applicable legislation (Law No. 133/2011 and, from August 2026, Law No. 195/2024), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: to request a copy of the data we process about you;
  • Right to rectification: to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure: to request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten), subject to legal conditions;
  • Right to restriction: to request restriction of processing in certain circumstances;
  • Right to data portability: to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on legitimate interest;

Withdrawal of consent: to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: info@applexium.com

We will respond to your request within 30 calendar days. In complex cases, the deadline may be extended by an additional 60 days, with prior notice to you.
