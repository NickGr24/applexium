Section 3

Consent and how to withdraw it

For the contact form we ask for your consent through a separate checkbox that is never pre-ticked. Together with your message we keep proof of that consent (policy version, consent text, date and time) so that we can demonstrate it under Art. 7(1).

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: an email to info@applexium.com is enough. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

Transfers outside the Republic of Moldova

Some of the recipients above process data in the European Union or the USA. Transfers are made only to countries ensuring an adequate level of protection or on the basis of the appropriate safeguards provided for in Art. 46 of Law 195/2024 (for example, the standard contractual clauses included in the providers' data processing agreements). You can request information about these safeguards at info@applexium.com.

Section 6

How long we keep data

  • Contact form messages and the proof of consent: no longer than 24 months from receipt, or less if the request does not lead to a contractual relationship;
  • Conversations with the Emmi assistant: as long as needed to answer your request;
  • Technical server logs: kept by the hosting provider under its own policy; we do not access them or link them to your identity;
  • Data in contracts or accounting documents: for the periods required by the laws of the Republic of Moldova.

When the period ends, the data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Section 7

Your rights

Under Law No. 195/2024 you have the right:

  • of access to your data and to receive a copy (Art. 15);
  • to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data (Art. 16);
  • to erasure (“right to be forgotten”) (Art. 17);
  • to restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • to data portability (Art. 20);
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise these rights, write to info@applexium.com. We reply within one month of receipt; for complex requests this may be extended by two further months, in which case we will let you know.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:

Section 8

Data security

SCALELAW SOLUTIONS SRL applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure, including:

In the event of a personal data breach we will notify the CNPDCP and, where required, the people affected, within the legal deadlines.
