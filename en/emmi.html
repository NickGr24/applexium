03 / CAPABILITIES

What Emmi can do

  • Speaks your customers' language — flawless Romanian and Russian on the phone; in chat it also replies in English and Ukrainian.
  • Reasons before it answers — understands what's actually being asked, even when the question is vague or spread across several messages.
  • Hears you perfectly — understands speech in under half a second, with no awkward silence.
  • Pronounces everything right — company names, phone numbers and abbreviations, spoken clearly every time.
  • See everything at a glance — what customers ask, on which channels, in which languages, live in your dashboard.
  • Turns conversations into leads — captures the contact and what they need, ready for your team or your CRM.
  • A summary every Monday — what happened, what was asked most, what needs your attention.
04 / GUARANTEES

What stands behind every answer

Answers in Under a Second

At the speed of a real conversation. The caller never waits in silence, and the chat never leaves anyone staring at a blank screen.

A Voice That Sounds Human

Warm and natural in Romanian and Russian, hard to tell from a real person — and it says company names, phone numbers and abbreviations correctly, every time.

Only From Your Documents

Every answer is grounded in what you uploaded. When the answer is not there, Emmi says so and hands the conversation to your team instead of inventing one.

Official Channel Connections

Phone, website, Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram are connected through each platform's own approved route — no workarounds that stop working overnight.

Smart Memory

Your documents become Emmi's memory — it finds the right information instantly, in either language.

Solid Foundation

Hosted in the EU and built to run around the clock — no breaks, no downtime surprises.
