01 / TRUST & DATA

Where the data lives and who can touch it

Everything below applies to Emmi, Legalia and Precedentia, and to the platforms we build for clients. No vendor names: we describe what happens to the data, not what the infrastructure is made of.

  • Hosted in the European Union

    Applications and databases are hosted in the European Union and built to run around the clock.

  • Every client, separate

    Each client’s data is isolated at the database level. No agent can see another client’s documents or conversations.

  • The documents stay yours

    Emmi answers only from the documents and links the client uploads. The knowledge base is edited by the client’s own team, with a history of every change: who, what and when.

  • Configurable retention, deletion on request

    How long conversations and recordings are kept is set per client. On request we export or delete the data, in line with GDPR.

  • Protection at the door

    Incoming messages are screened for attempts to manipulate the agent (prompt injection), and personal data in conversations can be masked.

  • Official connections, encrypted traffic

    Phone, website, Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram are connected through each platform’s official interfaces, with traffic encrypted in transit.

  • Access controlled by the client

    Each client manages its agent from its own dashboard: knowledge base, conversation history, leads, channels and analytics.

  • DPA and sub-processors

    We sign a data processing agreement (DPA) with every client who asks for one. The list of sub-processors is provided as part of that agreement.
