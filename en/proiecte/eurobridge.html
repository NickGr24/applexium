The platform of a Moldova–Ukraine grant programme, with support in four languages

The cross-border cooperation programme between Moldova and Ukraine needed a platform for applicants from two countries and a point of contact that answers in each applicant’s language. Applexium built eurobridge-uamd.org and connected Emmi for questions in Romanian, Russian, English and Ukrainian.

  • ClientEUroBRIDGE UA-MD
  • SectorEU cross-border cooperation programme
  • Year2026
  • Visit the platformeurobridge-uamd.org

Context

Applicants from two countries with questions about eligibility, deadlines and documents, in four languages. A small programme team that could neither answer everything in time nor afford a wrong answer.

What we built

  • The programme platform, with its documents, announcements and pages in several languages
  • Emmi on the site for applicants’ questions, in Romanian, Russian, English and Ukrainian
  • Answers only from the programme’s official guides and documents
  • Hand-over to the programme team for the cases that need a decision
