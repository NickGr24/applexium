02 / CONCEPT

Four courts, one search bar

Precedentia brings the case law of Moldova and Romania together in one place — four courts, one simple search bar. What used to take hours of digging through archives now takes seconds.

Search by keywords or describe your situation in your own words — the platform understands what you mean, not just what you type. The AI assistant answers legal questions with exact quotes from real decisions, and you can even attach a ruling to instantly find similar precedents.

All databases are automatically updated from official sources, ensuring practitioners always have access to the latest decisions.
